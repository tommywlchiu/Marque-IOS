Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2026

Marque ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights with respect to that information.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and a username of your choosing. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive limited profile information from those providers.

Vehicle information. Any car data you add to Marque — including make, model, year, VIN, license plate, mileage, service history, photos, insurance details, and registration dates — is stored on our servers and associated with your account.

Profile information. Your display name, username, bio, location, and profile photo are stored and used to populate your public profile if you choose to make it visible.

Driver license information. If you choose to save your driver's license number, state, and expiry date, this information is stored only on your device using local storage. It is never transmitted to our servers.

Usage data. We collect standard analytics data (screens viewed, features used, crash reports) through Firebase to improve the app. This data is not linked to your identity.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the Marque app
  • To send local push notifications for vehicle expiry dates and service reminders (these are scheduled on-device and do not go through our servers)
  • To display your public profile and public car listings to other Marque users, when you choose to make them public
  • To process and maintain your Pro subscription
  • To respond to your support requests

3. How We Store Your Information

Your account and vehicle data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Firebase Storage), hosted on Google's infrastructure in the United States. Firebase is SOC 2 Type II certified and encrypts data in transit and at rest.

Driver license information is stored only in your device's local storage and is never sent to our servers or any third party.

4. Information We Share

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Public content. When you set a car as public, its make, model, year, color, mileage, trim, photos, and service history (without costs or private fields) are visible to other Marque users in Explore and on your public profile. Your VIN, license plate, insurance details, registration expiry, and all costs are never made public.
  • Service providers. We use Google Firebase for authentication, database, and file storage; Apple for Sign in with Apple and subscriptions; and Smartcar for optional vehicle connectivity. Each operates under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the public.

5. Connected Vehicles (Smartcar)

If you choose to connect a vehicle via Smartcar, Marque receives read-only data from your vehicle (odometer reading, vehicle identification). This connection is made through Smartcar's OAuth flow. You can disconnect at any time from the Car Detail screen. Smartcar's privacy policy governs their handling of vehicle data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. Upon deletion, all your cars, maintenance records, photos, follow relationships, public listings, and account data are permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below or use the Delete Account feature in the app.

If you are a resident of California, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your data, and opt out of any sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

8. Children's Privacy

Marque is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the effective date and, where required by law, by sending you a notification within the app.
